### 1. SUMMARY AND OVERALL IMPACT STATEMENT

This manuscript presents RASCL (Rapid Assessment of Selection within CLades), an automated bioinformatics pipeline implemented in `Snakemake` and `[WORKFLOW_PLATFORM]` (Galaxy) for gene-by-gene comparative molecular selection surveillance of emerging viral lineages, applied here to SARS-CoV-2 variants of interest and concern (Alpha B.1.1.7, Gamma P.1, Delta B.1.617.2, Lambda C.37, Mu B.1.621, and Omicron BA.1, BA.2, and BA.5). RASCL extracts coding sequences for 21 viral genes/ORFs, downsamples query and global background sequences via `tn93-cluster` complete-linkage genetic distance clustering to a target size $D$ (e.g., 300–500 sequences per gene), reconstructs maximum-likelihood trees (`RAxML-NG` or `IQ-TREE`) with parsimony-labeled internal query and background branches, and executes nine `[SOFTWARE_PACKAGE]` codon/amino-acid analyses (`BUSTED[S]`, `SLAC`, `BGM`, `FEL`, `MEME`, `RELAX`, `Contrast-FEL`, `FADE`, and `FMM`) restricted to internal tree branches. Outputs are aggregated into JSON files for exploration in `ObservableHQ` dashboards. Code and workflows are publicly available.

Bundling `tn93-cluster` diversity-preserving subsampling with internal-branch-restricted codon selection models is a practical engineering strategy for scaling phylogenetic selection scans during a pandemic. However, the manuscript suffers from several substantive methodological, numerical, and quality-control flaws: (1) SARS-CoV-2 consensus genome artifacts—specifically tiled-amplicon primer dropout (`-` gaps) and reference-allele backfilling at clade-defining Omicron sites—are not filtered prior to selection inference, directly contaminating the highlighted BA.5 Spike results in Table 3 (`S/339`, `S/371`, `S/440`, `S/764`); (2) `BUSTED[S]` boundary optimization artifacts in Table 2 ($\omega_3 = 64,042.59$ with weight $p_3 = 0.00$ in Spike; $\omega_3 = 8,003.48$ with $p_3 = 0.00$ in `leader`, while $\omega_1 = \omega_2 = 1.0$) are reported as highly significant ($p \le 0.0002$) gene-wide adaptation without diagnostic scrutiny; (3) the interpretation of "Compression Ratio" in Table 1 is mathematically inverted in the caption and confounded by raw input sample size ($N_{\text{available}}$); (4) despite claiming results "within hours of being deployed," zero runtime or hardware benchmarks are provided; and (5) the Supporting Information captions for S8–S14 Tables (lines 255–279) contain widespread copy-paste errors labeling BA.5 and B.1.621 (Mu) tables as "BA.1 clade." These issues require rigorous sequence QC filtering for amplicon dropout and a thorough revision of the tables and text.

### 2. SPECIFIC CONCLUSIONS AND ASSESSMENT OF SUPPORT

* **`tn93-cluster` subsampling combined with internal-branch codon analyses enables automated execution of `[SOFTWARE_PACKAGE]` selection suites across 21 SARS-CoV-2 coding segments: [Well Supported].**  
  Restricting `FEL`, `MEME`, `BUSTED[S]`, and `Contrast-FEL` to internal branches of diversity-compressed alignments effectively reduces terminal-branch sequencing noise and keeps alignment sizes within tractable bounds ($N \sim 150\text{--}950$ per gene) for maximum-likelihood codon models.

* **Five highlighted sites in the BA.5 Spike gene (`S/339`, `S/371`, `S/440`, `S/764`, `S/1162` in Table 3) show strong statistical evidence of ongoing diversifying selection within the BA.5 clade: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  Inspecting the `Composition BA.5` column of Table 3 (lines 131–136) reveals that four of the five highlighted codons—`S/339` (`D327 G15 -8 N1`), `S/371` (`F310 S28 -9 L4`), `S/440` (`K298 N44 -9`), and `S/764` (`K325 N17 -8 I1`)—are canonical Omicron clade-defining substitutions (`G339D`, `S371F`, `N440K`, `N764K`) located in regions notorious for tiled-amplicon (ARTIC primer) dropout. Notice that each of these four sites contains 8–9 gapped sequences (`-8`, `-9`) alongside 15–44 apparent "reversions" to the ancestral Wuhan-Hu-1 reference residue (`G15`, `S28`, `N44`, `N17`). In public SARS-CoV-2 repositories, consensus assembly pipelines that fill low-coverage amplicon-dropout windows with the Wuhan-Hu-1 reference sequence produce recurrent spurious switches between the Omicron allele (`D339`, `F371`, `K440`, `K764`) and the ancestral reference allele (`G339`, `S371`, `N440`, `N764`) across internal branches of the phylogeny. `FEL` and `MEME` interpret these reference-backfill artifacts as 5–6 independent nonsynonymous substitutions ($p \sim 10^{-6}\text{--}10^{-7}$).

* **`BUSTED[S]` identifies statistically significant episodic diversifying selection ($q \le 0.1$) across 14 of 21 coding segments (66.7%) along internal branches of the BA.5 clade (Table 2): [Fragile].**  
  In Table 2 (lines 103–124), several segments flagged as highly significant ($p = 0, q = 0$ or $p = 0.0002$) exhibit degenerate rate-class parameter estimates indicative of numerical boundary behavior or alignment artifacts rather than robust episodic selection: for Spike (`S`, line 113), $\omega_3 = 64,042.59$ with rounded weight $p_3 = 0$; for `leader` (line 116), $\omega_1 = 1$ ($p_1 = 0$), $\omega_2 = 1$ ($p_2 = 1$), and $\omega_3 = 8,003.48$ ($p_3 = 0$). When $p_3$ rounds to $0.00$, the entire likelihood ratio test signal ($\Delta \text{LRT} \gg 15$) is driven by 1–2 outlier branch-site combinations (often a local frameshift, indel misalignment, or multi-nucleotide change) where the local synonymous rate $\alpha \to 0$ and $\beta/\alpha \to \infty$.

* **RASCL delivers selection reports "within hours of being deployed" and exhibits "lower compression ratios for Omicron" reflecting its "higher genomic variability" (Table 1): [Overstated].**  
  First, the manuscript contains no runtime measurements, CPU core counts, or wall-clock benchmarks to support the "within hours" turnaround claim. Second, in Table 1 (lines 71–81), the authors define `Compression Ratio` as `1 : (Available / Analyzed)` (e.g., `1:208` for BA.1 with 112,017 available and 524 analyzed; `1:152` for BA.2 with 48,623 available and 319 analyzed; `1:26` to `1:50` for non-Omicron lineages with 1,983–8,586 available). Because `tn93-cluster` adaptively tunes its distance threshold to cap the output at a fixed user-specified maximum $D$ (line 27), dividing `Available` by `Median analyzed` simply reflects how many raw genomes were downloaded from GISAID/ViPR ($112,017$ for BA.1 vs. $1,983$ for Delta), not intrinsic lineage variability. Moreover, the Table 1 caption states "The lower compression ratios for Omicron," whereas `1:208` and `1:152` are the *highest* compression ratios in the table (and several ratios do not match `Available / Median analyzed`, e.g., $1,983 / 59 = 33.6 \ne 29$ for Delta; $112,017 / 524 = 213.8 \ne 208$ for BA.1).

### 3. CONSTRUCTIVE AND DOABLE SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

#### 1. Mask Amplicon-Dropout Regions and Reference-Backfill Artifacts Prior to Selection Inference
* **WHY:** In SARS-CoV-2 lineages (especially Omicron BA.1/BA.2/BA.5), consensus genomes frequently suffer from primer dropout across Spike RBM/RBD amplicons, manifesting as stretches of `N`/gaps (`-8`, `-9` in Table 3) or erroneous reversion to the Wuhan-Hu-1 reference allele (`G339`, `S371`, `N440`, `N764`). These artifacts masquerade as recurrent parallel nonsynonymous substitutions on internal branches.
* **WHAT:** Implement a pre-alignment/post-alignment sequence QC filter in RASCL that masks or excludes sequences with local `N`/gap windows overlapping known amplicon-dropout regions, and test whether apparent reversions to the Wuhan reference allele co-occur across linked dropout sites within the same amplicon.
* **HOW:** Before running `RAxML-NG`/`IQ-TREE` and `[SOFTWARE_PACKAGE]`, scan each gene alignment for sequences carrying ancestral reference states at $\ge 2$ clade-defining positions within a single ARTIC amplicon tile (or flanked by `-`/`N` characters), mask those codons as `NNN`, and re-run BA.5 Spike `FEL`/`MEME`/`Contrast-FEL` to verify which sites in Table 3 remain significant.

#### 2. Audit `BUSTED[S]` Boundary Rate Estimates ($\omega_3 > 1,000, p_3 = 0$) Using `FMM` and Evidence Ratios
* **WHY:** In Table 2, `S` ($\omega_3 = 64,042.59, p_3 = 0$) and `leader` ($\omega_3 = 8,003.48, p_3 = 0$) achieve $p \le 0.0002$ despite $p_3$ rounding to zero, indicating that a handful of extreme outlier codon-branch events drive the gene-wide LRT.
* **WHAT:** Report $p_3$ to 4 significant digits (or scientific notation) in Table 2, inspect the site-level `BUSTED[S]` evidence ratios (ER) for `S` and `leader` to identify the exact codons driving $\omega_3 > 8,000$, and cross-check those codons against the pipeline's `FitMultiModel` (`FMM`) multi-hit output and gap locations.
* **HOW:** Use `[SOFTWARE_PACKAGE]`'s `BUSTED` JSON output (`Site Log Likelihood` / `constrained` vs. `unconstrained` evidence ratios) to filter out outlier codons with multi-nucleotide hits or adjacent frame-shifting indels, and report the filtered `BUSTED[S]` $p$- and $q$-values alongside Table 2.

#### 3. Correct the Table 1 Compression Ratios and Caption, and Add Wall-Clock Runtime Benchmarks
* **WHY:** Because `tn93-cluster` adjusts the distance threshold to return at most $D$ sequences (line 27), the ratio `Available : Median analyzed` is determined primarily by `Available` ($112,017$ for BA.1 vs. $1,983$ for Delta). Calling `1:208` a "lower compression ratio" than `1:29` in the Table 1 caption is numerically inverted, and the ratios `1:208` ($112,017/524 = 213.8$) and `1:29` ($1,983/59 = 33.6$) do not match the numbers in the table. Furthermore, the manuscript provides no timing data for the pipeline.
* **WHAT & HOW:** (1) Recompute the `Compression Ratio` column in Table 1 so it matches `Available / Median analyzed` (or clarify if it is the median of per-gene ratios), and report the `tn93-cluster` distance threshold $d$ tuned for each lineage (which actually measures sequence divergence); (2) fix the Table 1 caption; and (3) add a column or supplementary table reporting total wall-clock time and CPU-hours required to run RASCL on each lineage in Table 1.

#### 4. Fix Widespread Copy-Paste Errors in Supporting Information Captions (S8–S14 Tables)
* **WHY:** In the main text (lines 86–96 and 137), S8–S13 Tables correspond to **BA.5** and S14 Table corresponds to **B.1.621 (Mu)**. However, in the Supporting Information section (lines 255, 259, 263, 271, 279), the captions for S8, S9, S10, S12, and S14 Tables all erroneously state `"internal branches BA.1 clade"` or `"Ratio of omega (BA.1: background)"`. In addition, line 251 (S7 Table) defines `Omega1`, `Omega2`, and `Omega3` identically as `"refers to the first omega rate class"`.
* **WHAT & HOW:** Replace `"BA.1 clade"` with `"BA.5 clade"` in the captions for S8, S9, S10, and S12 Tables, replace `"BA.1 clade"` with `"B.1.621 (Mu) clade"` in the caption for S14 Table, and change `"first omega rate class"` to `"second"` and `"third"` for `Omega2` and `Omega3` on line 251.
